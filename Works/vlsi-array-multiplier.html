4x4 Hybrid Logic Array Multiplier

A full custom 4x4 unsigned array multiplier designed transistor by transistor in Cadence Virtuoso, using a hybrid Transmission Gate and Pass Transistor Logic full adder to cut transistor count while keeping full voltage swing at every node.

Full Adder 18T vs 28T CMOS
Team 4 Members

Fig 1. Top level multiplier assembled from AND, Half Adder, and Full Adder cells in Cadence Virtuoso.

Context & Objective

This was a group project for a VLSI course, built with three other members. The goal was to take a 4x4 unsigned array multiplier through a complete full custom design flow in Cadence Virtuoso, from transistor level schematics to a physically verified layout. I've always been interested in art, and naturally, took care of the layout designs for all modules.

Rather than using standard static CMOS adders, the design combines Transmission Gate Logic for full swing signal transfer with Pass Transistor Logic for compact carry generation. The result is a hybrid full adder built from 18 transistors instead of the usual 28, based on a structure adapted from a design proposed by Sadeghi et al.

Key Insights

Building Up From First Principles

Each building block, the AND gate, the half adder Sum and Carry paths, and the three module full adder, was designed and simulated on its own before being turned into a schematic symbol and reused hierarchically across the full 4x4 array.

Layout, DRC, LVS, Extraction

Every cell was laid out by hand in gpdk180, passed DRC with zero violations and LVS with a clean match, and the top level layout went through RC parasitic extraction in Quantus to confirm the multiplier still worked correctly with real interconnect delay included.
